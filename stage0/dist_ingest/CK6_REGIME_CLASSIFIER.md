## 9.2 Telemetry

Used by the classifier (from `di_node_metrics_t`):

- `read_wait_ns` (line 162): time in read/IO — source-limited signal.
- `publish_wait_ns` (line 163): time waiting to publish (queue full) —
  admission-limited signal.
- `consume_wait_ns` (line 164): consumer-side wait — demand/consumer signal.
- Backlog fields (`published_backlog`, per-node caps) — saturation proxy.
- `zero_claims` (line 160) — admission-denied telemetry.

## 9.3 Regime classifier

`classify()` in `tests/test_regime_ck6.py` implements a post-run classifier
using existing telemetry (no new hot-path counters). Categories:

| Regime | Evidence/interpretation |
|---|---|
| source-limited | read_wait high, backlog shallow, consume_wait low |
| admission-limited | publish_wait high or zero_claims high, backlog high |
| demand/consumer-limited | consume_wait high AND backlog >= max (saturation + starvation both required; high consume_wait alone means consumer STARVATION) |
| balanced | waits low, backlog not persistently saturated |

Consumer-wait semantics (corrected): `consume_wait_ns` accumulates time
blocked on `cv_can_pop` because the queue is EMPTY. High consume_wait =
consumers starved, NOT saturated. To claim demand/consumer-limited you
need BOTH high consume_wait (starvation) AND persistently high backlog.

Thresholds are derived from the run duration: define `run_seconds` (set by
the caller) and require waits to exceed a fraction of it; the classifier
reports ratios (wait / run_seconds).

Pairing identity: (scenario, mapping, workload, parameter_set, candidate,
trial) must be preserved when pairing per-node medians for regime R.

## 9.4 Decision rule (rental, CK-6.5 — predeclared)

- Candidate X beats `centralized_ingest` in regime R only when:
  - Paired comparison key: (candidate X, trial, node, regime) per-node
    consumed-bytes medians.
  - Minimum pairs per regime: ≥3 qualifying pairs.
  - Median improvement threshold: ≥15% (direction: candidate median >
    baseline median).
  - Invalid/incorrect trials excluded from the median.
  - Insufficient sample count → inconclusive (report).
- Threshold derived from existing repeatability evidence in the Experiment
  WO (median-of-3 + 5M-record scale, min 2s runs).
