## Wake-all abort guarantee (CK-2.2)

`di_engine_stop()` sets `eng->stop=1` then, per node, locks `n->mu` and
broadcasts BOTH `cv_can_pop` and `cv_can_push` — every blocked producer and
consumer wakes and re-checks `stop`. `di_ingest_thread`/`di_consumer_thread`
check stop at loop top (queue pop returns -1). Threads are joined in
`di_engine_destroy` (`pthread_join` lines 1350/1360). No child processes are
spawned (pure-thread engine); no orphans possible.

## Timeouts

The engine has no global heartbeat by design (CK-2.3). The authoritative
outer boundary is the process-group-aware runner timeout.
